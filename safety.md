# Safety

This is an experimental research prototype. It is **not** a medical device and has no regulatory clearance.

Before any contact with a human:

1. Battery power only. Do not connect USB, chargers or mains-powered equipment while electrodes are on skin.
2. Keep injected current very low (target well below 100 uA rms; verify with a calibrated meter on the phantom first, and consult applicable standards such as IEC 60601-1 / IEC 60601-1-11 for patient-applied parts).
3. Series DC-blocking capacitors on every electrode line; confirm no DC offset with a meter.
4. Use skin-safe hydrogel electrodes on intact skin only. Stop if irritation occurs.
5. Get review by someone qualified in medical electronics, and ethics approval for any formal human study.
6. Do not use outputs to make health decisions.
